## Horizon and cadence

**Problemshift-counted, soft calendar.** An iteration closes on **resolution**, not on the clock. Research does not commit to a discovery, only to running the attempt. Nominal ~2 weeks per iteration, but the deliverable governs.

## Roles

- **Execution** (thinx-Claude plus spawned agents): retrieval, parsing, scripting, structural moves, link repair, belt drafting.
- **Approval gates** (JD): belt commitments, Tier 3 discriminator registration, the appraisal verdict, the progressive-versus-degenerating call, and ratifying the chronology partition. These are his by `morals.md` and by `research-practices.md` rule 11, and each package's `authority_boundary` names where its edge lies.

**Commitment semantics.** O1 through O4 are committed: bounded, mechanical, they will land. O5 and O6 are stretch, and the commitment is to **run the attempt to a recorded resolution, not to a positive result.** A recorded refutation closes a stretch objective successfully.

This semantics is deliberate. On 2026-08-17 a refutation was logged as a cost against the programme rather than as completed work, and the correction had to be issued the same day. The distinction is now structural.
