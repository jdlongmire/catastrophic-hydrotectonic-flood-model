> "ringwoodite in the MTZ should contain 1 to 2 wt % water. The MTZ should thus be nearly water-saturated globally."

## Caveats the authors themselves state, carried into this programme's claims rather than dropped

> The water-saturation inference is best constrained "under the regions that have experienced postglacial rebound" — global extrapolation is the authors' own inference, not a direct global measurement.

> "The solubility of water in ringwoodite has been found to decrease with increasing temperature" — a potential limit on storage capacity beyond what this programme's water-ledger models.

> The authors acknowledge unresolved discrepancies with independent electrical-conductivity studies of the transition zone: "the exact reasons for these discrepancies are unclear and remain unknown."

> Experimental temperature-measurement uncertainty (~50 K) alone propagates to roughly a factor-of-two uncertainty in the dislocation-mobility estimate.
