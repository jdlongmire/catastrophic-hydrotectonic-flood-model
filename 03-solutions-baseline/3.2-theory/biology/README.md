## The historical architecture

$$\text{designed initial biodiversity} \rightarrow 50\text{–}60\text{ ka diversification} \rightarrow \text{Fall ecological transition} \rightarrow \text{continued diversification} \rightarrow \text{Flood bottleneck/filter} \rightarrow 5\text{–}6\text{ ka radiation}$$

An explicit pre-Fall ecological reading accompanies it: ordinary non-human death,
decomposition, nutrient cycling, and speciation are permitted; predation, harmful parasitism,
and pathogenic exploitation are excluded pre-Fall. This is a theological/biological claim
inherited from hard-core commitment 4's DFM import — **not derived or sourced here**, and
flagged for whoever formalizes it that it needs its own literature pass, separate from this
file.

**Why this reframes the problem rather than solving it.** The prior implicit burden — generate
observed biodiversity from a bottleneck in ~4,500 years — is not the actual research problem
this architecture poses. The real question is whether observed mutation, recombination,
selection, population dynamics, and speciation rates can generate the required diversification
delta across **two** windows: ~50–60 ka pre-Flood (unconstrained by any bottleneck) and the
post-Flood bottleneck-to-radiation window (~5–6 ka, constrained by a small founding population
per kind). That is a large, well-defined population-genetics project. **It has not been
started** — no rate literature has been checked, no population model built, no delta computed
for even one clade.

## Layering — most of this is not Hydrotectonic's to own

This is the sharpest layering case in the whole gap ledger. Per
[`../../3.1-hypothesis/hard-core.md`](../../3.1-hypothesis/hard-core.md)'s third disclosure:

- **Designed initial biodiversity and the pre-Fall ecological reading are DFM's variables** —
  parallel to $R_0$ in [`../stratigraphy/README.md`](../stratigraphy/README.md) §The broader
  question. This programme consumes them as a boundary condition, it does not derive them.
- **The ~50–60 ka pre-Flood diversification-rate modeling and the ~5–6 ka post-Flood
  radiation-rate modeling belong to sibling programmes** — pre-Flood diversification is closer
  to DFM's territory (it proceeds from DFM's initial state, under ordinary law, before this
  programme's mechanism engages at all); post-Flood radiation belongs to Post-Flood Recovery
  Models, alongside the deglaciation and tectonic-relaxation calculations in
  [`../cryosphere/README.md`](../cryosphere/README.md).
- **What is actually this programme's job:** the bottleneck/filter mechanism itself — survival
  through the catastrophic transition. Which organisms, how many founders per kind, and by what
  *physical* mechanism they cross F1–F4 (the phase schema,
  [`../stratigraphy/README.md`](../stratigraphy/README.md)) is a question this repo's own
  Flood-year mechanics actually bears on: habitat destruction timing, the freshwater/salinity
  refugia mechanism ([`../water-ledger/README.md`](../water-ledger/README.md) §Salinity
  refugia), and the translation/deposition sequence itself. Everything upstream of the
  bottleneck and everything downstream of the radiation is not this repo's calculation to run.
