# 03-solutions-baseline/

**The programme itself.** The Lakatosian ladder, preserved from the pre-refactor tier layout as sub-numbering (see [`ADR-HTF-META-0001`](../00-meta-model/ADR-HTF-META-0001-adopt-vwmm-convention.md)).

| Rung | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [`3.1-hypothesis/`](3.1-hypothesis/) | the **hard core** — immune *by decision*, not by demonstration |
| [`3.2-theory/`](3.2-theory/) | the **protective belt** — kinematics, rheology, stratigraphy, water-ledger |
| [`3.3-prediction/`](3.3-prediction/) | the **appraisal** and the **discriminators** |

Read them in that order on a first pass. The belt is where the programme does most of its work and takes most of its pressure; the appraisal is where it is honest about what that pressure has cost.
