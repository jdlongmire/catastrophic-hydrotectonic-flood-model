# Programme Appraisal (Progressive vs Degenerating)

> The Lakatosian self-audit. A research programme is **progressive** if it predicts novel facts and some corroborate; **degenerating** if it only accommodates facts post hoc. This file is the standing answer to: *which is the hydrotectonic Flood model, right now?*

## Current verdict

**Too early to appraise. Structure in place; no discriminator yet evaluated; three accommodation moves recorded in the initial research.**

**Recalibrated 2026-08-19, JD-directed — wording only, no standard relaxed.** This header previously read *"Unappraised. Zero theoretically progressive steps taken. Three degenerating accommodation moves recorded."* Every fact in that sentence remains true and is retained below. What changed is that the phrasing led with a count that reads as a **verdict on the model** when it is a description of its **age**, and it did so on a scorecard the rival is never handed. Both corrections follow [`research-practices.md`](../../04-construct/methods.md) **rule 4a** (score the rival on the same card — same standard *and* same freedom) and are recorded per rule 6 rather than silently rewritten.

Two things make the recalibration warranted rather than generous:

- **Lakatos's own framework has no "failing" category for a programme this young.** MSRP's protective belt exists precisely so infant programmes are not strangled before their positive heuristic has been worked out; "not yet progressive" is the expected state at this stage, not an indictment. This programme is days from stand-up.
- **The rival is not scored on this card either.** By the same measure — claims pre-registered and then corroborated, as against accommodated after observation — much of historical geology's account of any *particular* basin or formation is retrodictive. That is not a criticism of it; it is what historical science mostly is. But it means "zero pre-registered corroborations" cannot function as a discriminating verdict when it goes unmeasured on the other side.

**What has not changed, and is not going to.** The programme keeps its high internal standard in full — the four discriminator conditions, the twenty-two research practices, the self-audit that killed its own K-Pg ichnofabric candidate and retained the refuted F3 framing in code. Per [`../../04-construct/methodology.md`](../../04-construct/methodology.md), that standard is *"not relaxed here because the subject matter — terrestrial geology rather than cosmology — is more empirically exposed, not less."* It is the programme's chief asset against every rival Flood model, and nothing in this recalibration touches it.

**The substantive position, stated without the scorecard framing.** The programme has an articulated hard core ([`../3.1-hypothesis/hard-core.md`](../3.1-hypothesis/hard-core.md)) and an articulated positive heuristic ([`ROADMAP.md`](../../01-strategic-baseline/1.2-strategy/roadmap.md)), and its founding transcript identified its own most consequential gating problem (the rheology/heat budget — see [`discriminators.md`](discriminators.md)) before any critic did. Three computational results have landed (`WP-HTF-0003`, `0006`, plus the energy-partition bounds), one candidate discriminator has been killed on its own conditions, and three unblocked packages are seeded. **It has not yet made a claim that forbids an observation and has had that claim checked** — which is what would move this verdict, and is the only thing that would.

## What would move the needle to *progressive*

A claim that forbids an observation ordinary historical geology permits — or permits one ordinary historical geology forbids — stated with its evaluation protocol in advance, and then corroborated. The two live candidates from the audit above (ecological-province co-occurrence; cross-basin propagation-front coherence) are the nearest routes, gated on ROADMAP items 3–4.

## What would mark *degeneration* — to be recorded as plainly as progress would be

- A fourth boundary redraw with the same shape as the first three: triggered by an anomaly, absorbing it, adding no forbidding content.
- The chronology partition (ROADMAP item 3) never getting fixed and dated — a moving target that's never pinned down is a programme that can't be wrong by construction.
- The rheology gate staying open indefinitely while belt work on kinematics and stratigraphy keeps accumulating around it, unaddressed.
- Citing the MTZ-hydration/subduction correlation as support without ever sharpening it past "the pattern is already known and compatible with our model."
