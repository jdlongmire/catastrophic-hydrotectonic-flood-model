### The retrospective finding

**PI-01's objective set did not govern PI-01's execution.** Roughly 60% of the increment's committed value went undelivered while a comparable or larger volume of unplanned work landed. That is not a complaint about the unplanned work, which was largely JD-directed and largely better than what it displaced. It is a statement that the plan was not functioning as a plan.

The displacement is **not random**, and that is the part worth carrying forward:

- The three undelivered committed objectives — O2 (dependency graph), O4 (chronology stamp), O8 (paper revision) — are the **mechanical, structural, unexciting** ones.
- Everything that landed unplanned was **research-interesting**: a new criterion, a new calculation, a rival's claim graded, a figure improved.
- **O4 is specifically the counterweight the appraisal names against its own degeneration marker** — *"the chronology partition never getting fixed and dated: a moving target that's never pinned down is a programme that can't be wrong by construction."* Deferring O4 for an increment is not neutral; it is the degeneration marker accruing, and it has now accrued once with the reason recorded.

PI-02 is shaped against this finding: a smaller committed set, the structural items placed first rather than last, and an explicit opportunistic budget so that JD-directed mid-increment work is planned-for rather than displacing.
