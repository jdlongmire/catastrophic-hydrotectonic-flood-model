# PI-02 Plan — Catastrophic Hydrotectonic Flood Model

> **Program Increment 2. Status: PROPOSED** — drafted 2026-08-19, pending JD's ratification of the objective set and of the two decisions named under [Open decisions](#open-decisions). Predecessor: [`PI-01.md`](PI-01.md), closed 2026-08-19.

> Cadence convention inherited from PI-01: **problemshift-counted, soft calendar.** An increment closes on resolution, not on the clock.

## The headline

**PI-02's spine is a single question: can this programme evaluate one discriminator against real data, either way?**

Every other objective here is either what unblocks that, what protects the programme from degenerating while it tries, or what stops the published record drifting further from the belt in the meantime. The standing verdict — *too early to appraise; no discriminator evaluated against data* — has held since stand-up, and per [`appraisal.md`](../../03-solutions-baseline/3.3-prediction/appraisal.md) an evaluated discriminator is **the only thing that would move it.** Two candidates are now reachable; PI-01 reached neither.

A recorded refutation closes this spine successfully. A recorded *"the data cannot decide"* also closes it, provided the missing quantity is named per rule 18. What does not close it is another increment of belt construction, however good.

## Roles — unchanged from PI-01

- **Execution** (thinx-Claude plus spawned agents): retrieval, parsing, scripting, structural moves, link repair, belt drafting.
- **Approval gates** (JD): belt commitments, Tier 3 discriminator registration, the appraisal verdict, the progressive-versus-degenerating call, ratifying the chronology partition, and authorizing any publish. Each package's `authority_boundary` names its own edge.

## How this increment is shaped differently, and why

PI-01's close-out records the pattern this plan answers: **the mechanical structural objectives lost, every time, to the research-interesting ones.** That ordering was mostly right on epistemic value and is not treated here as a failure — an earlier draft of the close-out scored it as a delivery shortfall, and that framing was withdrawn 2026-08-19 as the same asymmetry rule 4a corrects. What remains is the observation that the structural items do not become less necessary by losing, and that **one of them, O4, carries a cost on the programme's own self-imposed degeneration markers** rather than on any comparison.

Three structural changes follow, none of them a restriction on JD's ability to direct work mid-increment:

1. **Fewer committed objectives.** Three, not six — matched to what an increment of this kind actually carries alongside directed work, rather than to what a day-one plan can list.
2. **The structural items are placed first, not last.** P1 and P2 are the two that lost PI-01. They are sequenced ahead of the research spine rather than beside it.
3. **An explicit opportunistic lane with a budget.** PI-01's unplanned work was good work, and most of it was better than what it displaced; the problem was only that nothing counted it. The lane below reserves capacity up front and requires that what lands there be *recorded against the lane* rather than silently absorbed.

### Why P1 is first and why deferring it again would cost something specific

`appraisal.md` names four degeneration markers. One of them is this objective by name: *"the chronology partition never getting fixed and dated — a moving target that's never pinned down is a programme that can't be wrong by construction."*

PI-01 deferred it once, for reasons that are recorded and were mostly good. **A second deferral is the marker firing twice**, and if PI-02 closes with P1 unstarted that belongs in the appraisal log as an entry against the programme's own interest, not as a scheduling note. Stated here in advance so it cannot later be argued about after the fact — the same discipline rule 1 imposes on a computation.

The chronology axis is also where PI-01's own structural risk concentrated: radiometric ages read as residuals, rhythmites as rapid laminae, ice cores likely third. Three chronometer reinterpretations and nothing date-stamped is the exact shape the marker describes.

### Why P2 is worth more now than it was in PI-01

The belt grew substantially in PI-01 — two registered exclusions, four new falsification conditions (F10–F13), a forbidding band on shear-zone geometry, a phase schema, and a four-tier architecture spanning sibling programmes. **The graph P2 declares is now checking a materially larger and more interconnected structure than the one it was scoped against.**

This is also the programme's own circularity check made mechanical. `mind.md`'s standing discipline requires a dependency graph be acyclic for any derived-parameter work; P2 is where that stops being a habit and becomes a script.

### Why P3 escalated from value 5 to 6

The published record (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21972859) is now out of step in more places than the velocity inconsistency it shipped with:

- The **title** names the "Global" model; the programme renamed 2026-08-18.
- **§3.4**'s velocity inconsistency is closed by `WP-HTF-0003` and the paper does not know it.
- **§6.2** treats K-Pg as a tested-and-rejected boundary, which contradicts the hard core's Chicxulub-in-swarm commitment.
- The paper predates **E1 and E2**, the forensic re-base, the retirement of "Araratia" in favour of Pangaea, and the F12 shear-zone band.

A citable record disagreeing with its own programme in five places is a bigger liability than one disagreeing in one place. The counter-argument is real and is recorded rather than dismissed: republishing during an increment that may itself change the belt again risks a v0.3 close behind. **P3's package carries that as its `gating_decision`; the call is JD's.**

### Why P6 is new, and why it is the highest-leverage stretch item

ROADMAP item 1 — the rheology/heat budget — is stated as *the gate on everything*, and its lateral half closed in PI-01 (`WP-HTF-0006`). **Its vertical half has not moved since stand-up, for one reason: no channel geometry exists anywhere in the belt.**

That single missing quantity blocks at least three separate things:

- the vertical heat term of ROADMAP item 1 (the gate itself),
- ROADMAP item 11b, applying Kirby's olivine deformation map at subduction-channel conditions — which is what would test whether the gate's "3–4 orders short" verdict is a category error,
- and `WP-HTF-0012`'s F9, which named the same missing quantity on the lateral side before F12 supplied its band.

Nothing else in the current inventory unblocks three items at once. P6 is stretch rather than committed because the honest outcome may be *"the literature does not constrain this,"* which is a legitimate close under rule 18 and a real result — it would convert the gate from *unquantified* to *unquantifiable from current sources*, which is a different and more useful thing to know.

## The opportunistic lane

**Budget: roughly 30% of increment capacity.** Reserved for JD-directed work that arrives mid-increment. This is a plan for it, not a limit on it — JD can direct anything at any time, and the lane exists so that when he does, the displacement is visible rather than silent.

**The one rule:** work landing in this lane is recorded against the lane in this file's close-out. If it exceeds the budget, the close-out says so and says which objective it displaced. That is the whole mechanism — PI-01's failure was not that unplanned work happened, it was that nothing counted it until the increment was over.

## Sequencing

```
P1 chronology ──┐  (first: the marker that fired once already)
P2 dep-graph ───┤
                ├──► belt is pinned and checked, then:
P6 channel geometry ──► ROADMAP 1 vertical half
                   └──► ROADMAP 11b (Kirby deformation map)

P4 candidate 4 (FWI) ── reachable now, blocked on nothing
P5 candidate 8 ──────── reachable in method, BLOCKED on retrieval

P3 paper v0.2 ──► [JD authorizes publish]
```

**P4 and P5 do not wait on P1/P2.** They are independent and they are the spine; sequencing the structural items first is about where attention defaults when both are open, not about a hard dependency. If P4 becomes runnable early it runs early.

**P5's block is retrieval, not method** — Lee 2021 behind a Cloudflare JS challenge, Larson et al. 2016 at HTTP 403 on tandfonline. The escalation ladder for PI-02, in order, per rule 13: direct PDF paths on further institutional-repository mirrors; ResearchGate and author self-archived copies; the SSRN preprint's own mirror set; and — **needing JD's authorization, since it is outbound external correspondence** — a request to the author. If the ladder is exhausted, P5 closes as *retrieval dead, recorded*, which is a legitimate close and frees the value for elsewhere.

## Open decisions

Two calls belong to JD and are named here rather than assumed:

1. **Does P3 publish inside this increment, or wait?** Republishing now fixes five disagreements; republishing at the end of PI-02 risks fewer versions but leaves the record wrong for longer. The package carries the argument both ways.
2. **Is the ~19 GB Schouten et al. supplementary retrieval worth spending on, given rule 23c?** Tomography is an inversion, so P4's evidential weight is lower than it looked when the dataset was found, and pre-registered outcome (c) — *resolution cannot decide* — is live and known in advance. The case for spending anyway: it is the only discriminator this programme can currently evaluate against data at all, and (c) is itself a recorded result.

A third, smaller one: **`ADR-HTF-META-0001` has been *Proposed* since 2026-08-18** and wants an accept or a reject.

## The honest standing at PI-02 open

**Too early to appraise. No discriminator evaluated against data.** Unchanged from PI-01 open, and that is the fact this increment exists to change.

What *has* changed since PI-01 opened is worth stating alongside it, because it is the reason the spine is now reachable at all: the programme holds two registered exclusions where it held none, it has a forbidding band on shear-zone geometry, it knows its own genre is forensic rather than hypothetico-deductive, and it has a research-practice layer that has already cost it something — rule 23c weakened its own candidate 4, and rule 24a exposed a real limit in candidate 7. A method layer that only ever strengthens the programme is decoration; this one is not.

None of that is a discriminator evaluated against data.
