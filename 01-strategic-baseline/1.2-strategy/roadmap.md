# ROADMAP — the Positive Heuristic

> Lakatos's **positive heuristic**: the articulated plan for developing refutable variants of the core, not a reactive patch applied each time evidence bites. This programme's plan, ordered by what gates what.

## The gate on everything: rheology / heat budget

Every kinematic and stratigraphic claim in this programme assumes catastrophic-interval transient viscosity in the 10¹³–10¹⁴ Pa·s range is achievable via water-weakening plus thermal-runaway feedback. It has never been shown to be — only asserted as the qualitative direction a feedback loop would need to run. Until a quantitative case is made (or the requirement is shown unreachable, which would itself be a real result, not a failure), everything downstream is conditional. This is not one item among several; it is what makes every other item worth doing.

## Sequencing note

The natural instinct is to build the belt out fully — kinematics, then rheology, then water ledger, then stratigraphy — and arrive at prediction with a mature apparatus. The Lakatosian instinct, per the sibling programmes, is the opposite: look for something that forbids a result early, even in crude form, because a programme that cannot say what would refute it has not yet begun, however much machinery it accumulates. Item 1 is placed first for exactly that reason — it is the one place in the current material where the answer could come back "no," and a programme this early needs at least one place where that's a live possibility.
