# The programme's genre is forensic, and the discriminator standard was the wrong shape

**2026-08-19, JD-directed.** *"This is a forensics exercise and model."* Researched in primary rather
than accepted as a framing note, and the result re-bases this programme's core evaluative standard.

**Primary source, retrieved and read in full:** Cleland, C.E. (2001), *"Historical science,
experimental science, and the scientific method,"* **Geology 29(11): 987–990.** University of
Colorado Boulder, Dept. of Philosophy and Center for Astrobiology. Confidence **HIGH** — full text
extracted, all quotations below verbatim from it. Builds explicitly on Chamberlin, T.C. (1897),
*"The method of multiple working hypotheses,"* *Journal of Geology* 5:837–848.

## What Cleland establishes

**The thesis, verbatim:**

> "When it comes to testing hypotheses, historical science is not inferior to classical experimental
> science. Traditional accounts of the scientific method cannot be used to support the superiority of
> experimental work."

**Why — the asymmetry of overdetermination.** Causation runs one way in time, so *"localized present
events overdetermine their causes."* A past event leaves far more traces than are needed to establish
it, which is why erasing all evidence of a crime before committing it is so hard. Cleland: *"a
remarkably small subcollection of traces is enough to confer at least high probability on the
occurrence of a past event, and that there are likely to be many such subcollections."*

**The method that follows.** Not controlled repetition. Historical scientists *"usually concentrate on
formulating multiple competing hypotheses about particular past events. Their main research efforts
are directed at searching for a smoking gun."*

> **"A smoking gun is a trace that picks out one of the competing hypotheses as providing a better
> causal explanation for the currently available traces than the others."**

**Falsificationism is rejected as descriptive of practice** — in a mainstream geology journal, not a
partisan source. Her Neptune case: facing a failed Newtonian prediction on Uranus's orbit, astronomers
*"didn't behave like good falsificationists,"* rejected an auxiliary assumption instead, and
discovered Neptune. *"Rejecting a hypothesis in the face of a failed prediction is sometimes the wrong
thing to do."* This bears on the Duhem-Quine material in
[`20260818-deep-time-parsimony-debate-notes.md`](20260818-deep-time-parsimony-debate-notes.md), and it
cuts symmetrically — which is what makes it usable rather than a convenience.

**Pre-registration is explicitly not the standard.** Her paradigm case of a successful smoking gun is
the Alvarez K-Pg iridium anomaly, and she notes it *"was not predicted in advance of its discovery."*
Sometimes *"one just gets lucky and stumbles over a smoking gun."*

**But the discipline is real and the failure mode is named:**

> **"Failure to search for a smoking gun deprives a historical hypothesis of empirical grounding,
> turning it into a dreaded just-so story."**

## What this changes here, and what it does not

The four discriminator conditions in
[`../03-solutions-baseline/3.3-prediction/discriminators.md`](../03-solutions-baseline/3.3-prediction/discriminators.md)
were Popperian: forbids an observation, rival permits it, evaluable, failure condition stated first.
That is experimental-science shape applied to a historical-science problem. Re-based against Cleland:

| Old condition | Disposition |
|---|---|
| 1. **Forbids** an observation | **Reframed.** The historical unit is not "forbids" but *discriminates among a stated hypothesis set* — Cleland's smoking gun. Exclusion remains valuable in its own right (see below) |
| 2. **The rival permits it** → discriminates nothing | **Kept, and promoted to the core.** This is already the smoking-gun criterion stated in the negative. Nothing changes; it becomes the centre rather than one of four |
| 3. **Evaluable** against reachable data | **Kept, sharpened by rule 23c** — the trace must be an observation, not a model output |
| 4. **States failure condition first** | **Demoted from gate to discipline.** Cleland's own paradigm smoking gun was not predicted in advance. Retained as good practice against post-hoc rationalisation, no longer a bar to registration |

**And one requirement is added that this programme did not previously have.** Chamberlin's multiple
working hypotheses is not optional under this framing — a smoking gun is defined *relative to a stated
set of competing hypotheses*. Registering a discriminator now requires naming the full candidate set
it discriminates among, not just this programme and "ordinary historical geology" as a monolith. **The
re-base therefore adds an obligation while removing one that was genre-inappropriate. It is not a
loosening.**

**Exclusion is a first-class product, not a consolation prize.** Forensic practice values excluding a
suspect as highly as identifying one, and often holds it more securely. That reframes what this
programme has already produced: the **vapor ceiling** (~0.46 m, no vapor-mediated mechanism can supply
the 40-day rain) and the **enthalpy ceiling** that killed Baumgardner's steam jets are exclusions —
genuine forensic results, and under the old framing they had no natural category.

## Verification that the re-base does not resurrect anything

A methodology change that conveniently revives one's own dead candidates is exactly what a critic
should suspect, so this was checked explicitly rather than assumed.

**The K-Pg ichnofabric candidate stays dead.** It was killed on condition 2 — both accounts permit
bioturbation. Under the smoking-gun criterion the verdict is identical and arguably sharper: a trace
both hypotheses predict *is not a trace that picks out one of them*. It also still fails Cleland's
own standard, since the discriminating literature was already published before the claim was framed.
No resurrection.

**Candidate 4 is weakened, not strengthened.** Tomographic texture is a model output (rule 23c), so it
is a poor smoking gun on Cleland's own terms — the trace must be a trace. **Candidate 8 is
strengthened**: a globally synchronous overflow-breach regime across 1,549 transverse drainages is
smoking-gun shaped, and gap morphology is genuinely observational.

## One thing to be precise about, because it would be easy to overclaim

Cleland's paradigm smoking gun is the K-Pg iridium and shocked quartz, and this programme declares
Chicxulub a swarm member. That is a striking alignment, **but the iridium points to an impact, which
both accounts accept.** It discriminates on the surrounding chronology not at all. Citing Cleland's
example as support for this programme specifically would be exactly the error rule 4a exists to
prevent. What the alignment gives is a *methodological* precedent for the kind of trace worth hunting
— not evidential support.
