# Deep-time parsimony debate — verified notes and methodology

**Status: reference material for future debate/defense work, not belt content.** Does not touch
the hard core, belt, or appraisal verdict — nothing here is a Hydrotectonic mechanism claim.
Captured because it produced (a) genuinely verified, sourced findings worth reusing in future
public defense of the programme, and (b) a methodological correction worth keeping visible so it
doesn't have to be relearned.

## Origin

## The methodological error, caught mid-thread

First pass at the parsimony ledger applied `research-practices.md` rule 4 ("state the rival at
its strongest") asymmetrically — scrutinizing the ledger's claims about deep time hard while
letting mainstream rebuttals I cited in response pass unexamined. JD caught this directly:
*"you lean towards latitude towards the deep time auxiliary hypothesis."* Correct and specific.

**The general lesson, worth keeping for any future symmetric debate audit:** a weakness in one
theory's account of some evidence does not automatically transfer as strength to a rival
theory's account of the same evidence. Bayes compares $P(E \mid H_1)$ against $P(E \mid H_2)$ —
the same evidence, under both hypotheses. Lowering confidence in $H_1$'s specific explanation
does not by itself raise $P(E \mid H_2)$; that term has to be earned separately, by $H_2$ making
its own checked, quantitative prediction of the same evidence. Cataloging a rival's explanatory
friction and treating the alternative as having won by default is a likelihood-ratio conflation,
not a completed Bayesian comparison — and it's the same category error regardless of which
theory it's run in defense of.

**Applied to this programme specifically:** the Hydrotectonic belt's own appraisal states zero
discriminators evaluated. None of the anomalies below (soft tissue, C-14 residuals, isochron
scatter) have a corresponding checked, quantitative prediction anywhere in this belt. A real
posterior shift against deep time's specific explanations in a given domain (confirmed for two
of four items below) does not mechanically land on this programme unless this programme's own
account of that same evidence has been built and checked — which, honestly, it mostly hasn't.

## Verified findings, item by item (confidence labels per `research-practices.md` rule 2)

**Soft tissue / Fenton chemistry — mainstream mechanism weaker than initially credited.**
Schweitzer's iron/hemoglobin preservation experiment showed roughly 200-fold stability increase
(about 3 days to a little over 2 years, room temperature), extrapolated to 68 million years with
no intervening time-series data. A 2023 direct test (raw chicken bone, buried in various
preservative solutions) found iron performed *worst* among tested options after 90 days.
Confidence MEDIUM (search-summary level; primaries not independently read in full this pass).
This mechanism rests on the same unbridged rate-extrapolation move the "parsimony ledger" itself
criticizes deep time for making elsewhere — a real point in the ledger's favor on this specific
item, on inspection rather than assertion.

**Isochron mixing-line vulnerability — real, mainstream-documented, holds up.** Isochron dating
requires the sample suite to share a common initial daughter-isotope ratio; mineral-scale studies
of young volcanic rocks have shown this assumption invalid in some instances, and excess scatter
from partially-reset or multi-component systems can produce a statistically plausible but
non-age-bearing apparent isochron. Confidence MEDIUM-HIGH (multiple independent geochronology
sources, not creationist literature). A genuine methodological caveat on the rival account, not
a strawman.

**C-14 in coal/diamonds — genuinely contested, roughly a wash.** Atmospheric C-14 contamination
runs 2-3 orders of magnitude above sample levels in old material, and coal-specific contamination
(microbial/fungal activity in the substrate) is independently documented outside creationist
sources. The RATE (creationist) diamond study's own reported figures — 0.01 to 0.07 pMC after
background subtraction — are thin enough that residual background/procedural artifact remains a
live, quantitatively plausible explanation. Confidence MEDIUM. Neither side's account is clearly
winning on the actual reported magnitudes.

**Concordance base rate — real figures, more honest than the unsourced claim first made.**
Brent Dalrymple estimates 5-10% of radiometric results are discordant; other cited estimates run
15-20%. Roughly 80-95% concordance depending on source and sample type — genuinely the modal
finding, but "frequently agree, notably don't always" is the accurate statement, not "vast
majority" asserted without a citation. Confidence MEDIUM (search-summary level; the USGS National
Geochronological Database Concordance Metric study, 18,575 records, would be the primary worth
retrieving if this becomes load-bearing).

**Weathering paradox — this programme's own founding research contradicts the ledger's
stratigraphic claim, independent of the above.** The parsimony ledger's stratigraphic section
claims paraconformities "frequently show knife-edge flat contacts with no evidence of deep soil
horizons." This programme's own founding research tested Cambrian, P-T, T-J, and K-Pg boundaries
and rejected all four on the *opposite* finding — paleosols, coal, and reefs recurring past every
candidate boundary. This doesn't depend on which side is right about deep time; it's an internal
inconsistency between an external argument and this programme's own recorded fieldwork search,
and needs correcting before the ledger is used in any public defense of this programme.

## Ten-item stratigraphic-flexibility list — audited, not accepted flat

The list (reworking, bioturbation, infiltration, condensation, refugia, preservation failure,
sampling failure, taxonomic reassignment, range extension, Lazarus taxa) does not split evenly.
The real test for each: **is it independently diagnosable (physical evidence beyond the anomaly
itself), or can it be invoked purely to preserve the expected age with no independent check?**

- **Independently diagnosable** (legitimate, principled): reworking (abrasion, mineralization
  state, size-sorting), bioturbation (burrow structures, ichnofabric indices — this program's own
  K-Pg ichnofabric discriminator was killed exactly this way, on physical trace-fossil evidence),
  infiltration (visible root traces, fracture-fill), taxonomic reassignment (re-examination of
  the actual specimen), preservation/sampling failure (general taphonomic and completeness
  models, not invented per-case).
- **Genuinely soft** — the sharpest available target if this comes up in debate: **ecological
  refugia** invoked for an *unobserved* persistence during a gap, with no independent trace. When
  a relict population is actually found alive, that's real evidence; when posited purely to
  bridge a silent interval, it's a legitimate Duhem-Quine target.
- **Not really the same category**: range extension is ordinary data incorporation, not a rescue
  mechanism; Lazarus taxa describes the *pattern*, with the actual explanation usually appealing
  to preservation or sampling failure — counting it separately double-counts.

**Symmetric point, since the same standard applies to both sides:** this programme runs
functionally the same kind of apparatus (PPLC, salinity refugia, the phase schema's diachronous
boundary, "possible third position" readings), several tagged `forbids: none` in its own belt for
exactly this reason. The existence of auxiliary machinery isn't the tell on either side — what
would be damning is auxiliary machinery invoked *without* independent diagnostic criteria. The
Oard & Klevberg dispute over one specific paleosol identification is evidence *against* the
"unfalsifiable flexibility" reading generally: a dispute over correct application of a criterion
is only possible because the criterion is real and shared, not ad hoc.

**Tu quoque scope, clarified by JD:** the intended use of the ten-item list is narrower than a
decisive parsimony argument — it's ammunition for the specific case where someone tries to use
"the catastrophic model has auxiliary hypotheses" as a standalone disqualifier. For that purpose
the list holds (deep time has the same category of apparatus, refugia especially). Worth keeping
the caveat attached whenever this gets reused: a tu quoque neutralizes an asymmetric objection,
it doesn't itself establish which account is better supported. That decision runs on the
independently-diagnosable-vs-ad-hoc test above, not on the mere existence of auxiliary hypotheses
on either side.
