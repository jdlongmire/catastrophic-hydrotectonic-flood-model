## Continued same day: WP-HTF-0003 (velocity/detachment reconciliation) closed

After `WP-HTF-0009` landed, JD asked for a priority recommendation across the open packages.
Recommended `WP-HTF-0003` first — bounded, and a force-multiplier since it unblocks both
`WP-HTF-0006` (the actual rheology/heat-removal gate) and the recommended gating for
`WP-HTF-0008` (paper v0.2) — with `WP-HTF-0005` (candidate 8 retrieval) flagged as a parallel
track, not a competing priority, since it's blocked on access rather than compute. JD confirmed;
`WP-HTF-0010` (ice-age energy balance) was seeded first as a small side item.

**What was worked.** `02-theory/kinematics/code/velocity_detachment_reconciliation.py` —
resolved the belt's oldest internal inconsistency (three disagreeing velocity figures, two
disagreeing detachment depths) using this programme's own parameters, not the disputed source's
block dimensions.

**What confirmed.** Driving force is isostatic/PE-release (the source's own downslope-gravity
model can't initiate sliding on its own stated numbers — $\tan(0.1°) < \mu = 0.01$). Detachment
depth 15 km beats 50 km (above Moho, less extreme pore-pressure requirement). The programme's
existing 9–40 cm/s velocity figure survives, passing the F2 energy ceiling with a 15× margin.
The disputed 30 m/s figure is rejected on a third independent ground.

**What was surfaced before being written down (rule 11).** Solving for the pore-pressure ratio
the mechanism requires gave $\lambda = 0.999995$ to $0.9999996$ — five to six nines, past
typically documented overpressure ratios. Reported with two live readings rather than resolved
unilaterally: a genuine mechanism feature, or a symptom the imported friction allocation needs
revisiting. **JD's ruling: fine-tuning is not an issue in this model** — the hard core already
holds the Flood as a real, singular, designed event (commitments 3–4), so an honestly-derived
extreme parameter value isn't read as an ad hoc rescue the way it would be under a naturalistic
hard core. Promoted to a new standing rule, `research-practices.md` rule 21 ("Interpretive
posture") — general-purpose, not scoped to this one result, since the same question will recur
on `WP-HTF-0006` and `WP-HTF-0010`.

**What changed.** `research-practices.md` — new rule 21. `02-theory/kinematics/README.md` and
`POSITION-PAPER.md` Sec 3.4 — reconciliation written in, no unresolved inconsistency disclosed
