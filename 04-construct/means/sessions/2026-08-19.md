## Continuation 3 — rule 22 added, an asymmetric standard corrected

**JD's pushback:** *"naturalistic models routinely adjust knobs."*

He is right. Four free parameters in the new precipitation mechanism had been flagged as "knobs that
forbid nothing," with a proposed remedy that each be declared in advance or count as accommodation.
That framing was **asymmetric in exactly the way rule 4 exists to prevent** — a standard applied to
this programme that the rival does not meet either. ΛCDM fits six free parameters; the Standard Model
nineteen; stellar convection has used a fitted mixing length for a century; and **mantle viscosity
profiles, the quantity this programme's own rheology gate is benchmarked against, are inverted from
data rather than derived**.

**Rule 22 added** to `04-construct/methods.md`, replacing the stricter framing with the standard that
actually separates legitimate parameter freedom from ad hoc accommodation: **over-determination**. A
free parameter is a defect only when no evaluated constraint bears on it, and the measure is the
*ratio* of constraints to parameters, not the count. ΛCDM's six are legitimate because fixing them on
the CMB predicts BAO and supernovae and could fail; the mixing length is fitted on the Sun and carried
unchanged to other stars, where it remains a live problem. The knob is spent once, then has to survive
somewhere it was not fitted.

**Advance declaration demoted** from a requirement to a stopgap for a programme lacking
over-determination, to be dropped once constraints exist — not presented as a purity requirement, and
its absence not read as a defect.

**Rule 21 scope boundary added in both files.** Rule 21 governs a *derived* value landing at a
demanding point (`WP-HTF-0003`'s $\lambda$ at five nines — something the mechanism produced and owns).
It does **not** cover *free* parameters, which rule 22 governs. Reading rule 21 the second way would
make it a universal solvent and remove the programme's capacity to be wrong.

**The reframed diagnosis, which is the substantive consequence.** This programme is not carrying too
many knobs. It is carrying **zero evaluated constraints**. Different diagnosis, different remedy:
more constraints, not fewer parameters. Running `WP-HTF-0011`, `0012`, `0013` is the remedy.

**Note on what was corrected.** The error was mine, not the belt's. A double standard applied in the
programme's *disfavour* is as much a rule-4 failure as one applied in its favour; the symmetric
obligation runs both directions.

## Continuation 5 — rule 23: observation vs. historical interpretation

**JD:** *"And remember we're operating under historical interpretation, not direct observation,
don't make that category error."*

Third and widest of the day's method corrections. Both this programme and its rival reconstruct an
unobserved past from presently-observable evidence. What is *observed* is narrow and shared —
seismic waveforms, isotope ratios, mineral assemblages, layer counts, gap geometry, fossil position
in rock. Everything about *when* and *how* is inference on top.

**Rule 23, three parts.** (a) Label epistemic status — observation / measurement / historical
inference — as an axis distinct from rule 2's sourcing confidence, since a HIGH-confidence historical
inference is still an inference. (b) **The rival's chronology is not the yardstick, it is the other
hypothesis** — deep-time durations may show the rival *accommodates* an observation (condition 2),
never that an observation *refutes* a claim. This completes rule 4a: same standard, same freedom,
**same epistemic status**. (c) A discriminator must reduce to an observation, not to agreement with
a reconstruction; where the "data" is a model output, say so and name the observable beneath it.

**Bounded against relativism, explicitly.** "Both are interpretations" does not make both equally
supported. The shared observations constrain both accounts, and a discriminator is precisely where
two interpretations diverge *at the observation level*. Using this rule to dissolve unwelcome
evidence would be the mirror-image error and is disallowed as firmly as the one it corrects.

**Three retroactive corrections, forward not deleted.**

1. The Baumgardner cold-slab grading read as though thermal diffusion *observationally refuted* him.
   It cannot: 1-5 cm/yr and 58-289 Myr are the rival's **reconstruction**; what is observed is wave
   speed. What it legitimately shows is that the rival's own account accommodates cold lower-mantle
   slabs — sufficient for condition 2. Verdict stands, framing did not.
2. Schouten et al. is a correlation against **reconstructed** subduction — interpretation on both
   sides, now said so.
3. **F1-F9 are conditional consistency checks, not measurements.** Physics measured, inputs
   reconstructed. "F7 PASS" means *if this reconstruction is correct, the physics is consistent with
   it.* Now stated at the head of `rheology/README.md`, with the rule-4a note that the rival's
   equivalent model runs carry the same status.

**Candidate 4 caught by 23c, and it cuts against the programme.** Tomography is an inversion —
regularised, model-dependent, non-unique — so `WP-HTF-0011` now gates on showing any discontinuity
survives the inversion's own regularisation before it counts either way, and failing that *is* its
pre-registered outcome (c). This makes candidate 4 harder to close, and makes candidate 8's gap
morphology the more observationally grounded of the two reachable candidates despite its paywall.

## The three method corrections of 2026-08-19, in one line each

- **Rule 4a** — fixed *who* the standard is applied to (both sides, same standard and same freedom).
- **Rule 22** — fixed *what counts as a defect* (over-determination, not knob count).
- **Rule 23** — fixed *what kind of thing a claim is* (observation vs. historical inference).

All three were errors in how the method was applied by this programme's own analyst, not in the

## Continuation 6 — genre identified as forensic; discriminator standard re-based

**JD:** *"This is a forensics exercise and model."* Then, on the proposal: *"Follow your lean."*
My lean was to re-base, and I did.

**Researched in primary, not accepted as framing.** Cleland, C.E. (2001), *"Historical science,
experimental science, and the scientific method,"* **Geology 29(11):987–990** — full text retrieved
and extracted, all quotations verbatim, confidence HIGH. Builds on Chamberlin (1897), multiple
working hypotheses.

**The finding.** *"When it comes to testing hypotheses, historical science is not inferior to
classical experimental science."* The genre's method is not controlled repetition but multiple
competing hypotheses plus a search for a **smoking gun** — *"a trace that picks out one of the
competing hypotheses as providing a better causal explanation for the currently available traces
than the others"* — because the **asymmetry of overdetermination** means a past event leaves far more
traces than are needed to establish it.

**Two consequences.** Pre-registration is **not** the genre's standard: Cleland's paradigm case, the
Alvarez K-Pg iridium anomaly, *"was not predicted in advance of its discovery."* But the genre's
failure mode is sharper than falsification — *"failure to search for a smoking gun deprives a
historical hypothesis of empirical grounding, turning it into a dreaded just-so story."*

**Re-base landed** in `methodology.md` §Genre, `methods.md` rule 24, and `discriminators.md`
§What counts. Condition 2 promoted to the centre; "forbids" reframed as "discriminates" with
exclusion as a first-class result; "evaluable" sharpened by rule 23c; pre-registration demoted from
gate to discipline; **new condition added — name the competing-hypothesis set.**

**The bar went up, and I checked rather than asserted it.** Pre-registration removed as
genre-inappropriate, an explicit hypothesis set added that no existing candidate states (1, 2, 5, 6,
7 all now owe it). **No killed candidate resurrected** — the K-Pg ichnofabric kill was re-checked and
holds more sharply, since its fatal objection was always condition 2 and condition 2 is now the
centre. Candidate 4 **weakened** by re-scoring (tomography is an inversion); candidate 8
**strengthened** (gap morphology is a genuine trace).

**The substantive re-reading.** The vapor ceiling and the enthalpy ceiling that killed CPT's steam
jets are **exclusions** — first-class forensic products with no natural category under the old
framing. On this standard they are the most substantial evidential products the programme holds.
Materially different from "zero discriminators evaluated," with nothing lowered to get there.

**Overclaim flagged and refused.** Cleland's paradigm case is K-Pg and this programme declares
Chicxulub a swarm member — but iridium points to *an impact*, which both accounts accept, and
discriminates on chronology not at all. Methodological precedent, not evidential support. Citing it
as support would be the rule 4a error in its most tempting form.
